# Briar & Bone: How to Play

This is the full in-game guide (also available from the title screen and the pause menu under **How to Play**), written out for the repository.

## 1. The Basics

*You are the Reader. The Briarwood is a book that is trying to finish.*

**Touch:** drag anywhere on the **left half** of the screen to move (a thumbstick appears under your finger). Buttons sit on the right.

- **[A]** **Quill.** Tap to swing. **Hold** to charge, release for a spin attack. Face a person, sign, chest or ring and tap to **talk, read, open or use** it.
- **[S]** **Book.** **Hold** to raise your shield and block. You walk slower while it is up. Better books (Atlas, Dictionary, sold by Madam Thimble) reflect shots and block from the side.
- **[F]** **Flip** between the two worlds. See page 2.
- **[II]** The pause button (top right): your stats, jars, Wardrobe, this guide, and Save & Title.

**Keyboard:** WASD or arrows move · `Z` / Enter attack and interact · `X` shield · `Space` flip · Esc pauses. Tool keys are listed on page 4.


**Saving:** the game saves itself as you play (rooms, chests, bosses, everything). Use **Continue the Tale** on the title screen to come back.


> **Tip:** New buttons appear on screen only when you find the tool they belong to, so the screen stays calm at first.

## 2. Two Worlds

*Storybook is what the Book says. Truth is what happened.*

Every room exists twice. In the **Storybook** it is a fairytale, bright and tidy. In the **Truth** it is what the story was covering up: the paint peels, doors are different, and things that were safe may not be.


**Seams.** Shimmering cracks in the floor. Once you own the Mirror Shard, stand on a Seam and press `F` to step between worlds. Press it again on a Seam to come back. Later, the Shard grows into the whole Mirror (collect **Mirror Pieces**) and you can flip in more places.


**Look for what changes:** a bridge in one world is a pit in the other, a wall you cannot see through may be paint, a hedge may wilt, a ghost may only talk to you in the Truth, and some enemies are **harmless in one world and killable in the other**. A sword that bounces off a warden in the Storybook may bite in the Truth.


**Truth Memories.** Little remembered moments hidden in the Truth. Find them all.


> **Tip:** Stuck? Flip, look around, flip back. Almost every puzzle in the game is a question about which world you are standing in.

## 3. Fighting and Staying Alive

*Roses are your health. Farthings are your money. Neither lasts.*

**Roses** are hearts, shown top-left (each rose is four quarters). Find **Rose Containers** after bosses and fuse **Thorn Shards** (every four make a new rose) to grow them.


**Pink petals** from grass and foes restore a little health. Pause and drink a **Broth** jar to heal three roses.


**Weapons.** Your Quill can be upgraded at Madam Thimble's (Pen-Knife, Dagger, Calligrapher's Blade). Charged spin attacks hit everything around you.


**Enemies have rules.** The first time you meet a kind, a tip pops up. Frost Hounds need to be thawed with fire. Story Stalkers fear flame. Hedge Hounds fear the Sickle. Footmen need a certain key. Read the tips: they are not decoration.


**Bosses** have a pattern and a weakness. Watch what they do three times before you decide what is wrong. Mini-bosses guard many of your tools. If you fall, you wake at the last safe place and nothing is lost.


**Wish Jar.** Keep one and a fatal blow will leave you where you fell with half your roses.


> **Tip:** Fighting is meant to be learned, not ground. If a boss is too much, go and find another rose.

## 4. Tools of the Tale

*You earn these along the way. Each one opens doors you have already walked past.*
- **[W]** **Wheel of the Year.** Turns the season. Frozen things thaw, mud dries, beans sprout, leaves land. Face a soil patch and tap `A` to plant a bean.
- **[B]** **Gingerbombs.** Crack weak walls, startle rats. They blow after a moment, and that includes you.
- **[G]** **Braid.** Hooks onto hook posts and hauls you across gaps.
- **[R]** **Tinderbox.** Burns dry grass and thorn walls and lights braziers. Hounds of ice hate it.
- **[T]** **Cloak.** Glide over gaps. Different cloaks in the Wardrobe do different things.
- **[H]** **Mallet.** Smashes rubble.
- **[Y]** **Sickle.** Cuts hedges and leaves, and hurts hounds twice as much.
- **[I]** **Frost.** Freezes water and what stands in it.
- **[,]** **Shoes.** A short, fast dash. Shift on a keyboard.
- **[N]** **White Hart.** Call the Hart when a place is meant for it.
- **[O]** **Flute.** Tap to play the current song, hold to change songs. Rat Song, the Opening, and the Lullabies each have places they work.

Other gear works without a button: **Eagle Claws** climb handholds (only visible in the Truth), a **Selkie Coat** lets you swim, a **Lantern** clears fog, a **Hearthstone Mantle** keeps frost off you.


> **Tip:** Keyboard: Q Wheel · E Bomb · G Braid · R Fire · T Glide · H Mallet · Y Sickle · I Frost · Shift Shoes · N Hart · O Flute · C Blot.

## 5. Blot and Ink

*He is made of the unwritten. He is, technically, a dog.*

Early in the story you meet **Blot**, a small blot of ink who follows you. Once he trusts you he learns tricks. Press `K` (C on a keyboard) to use them. Every trick costs **Ink**, shown on your HUD.

•**Scribble** draws an ink bridge over a torn page in the floor.
•**Blot Out** erases false walls. **Hold K to Read**: suspicious things shimmer.
•**Stain** marks painted-over floor so it holds you in the Truth.
•**Carry** fetches an item across a gap. **Echo** sits on a pressure plate for you. **Ink Storm** (with the Sickle out) is a spinning attack.

**Inkwells** are hidden around the world. Every one restores your Ink, raises Blot's **Trust**, and later grows your Ink meter. There are 40.


> **Tip:** A Moonwater jar refills Ink anywhere from the pause menu.

## 6. Exploring and Collecting

*The map is bigger than the story, on purpose.*

**Rings.** Stone circles scattered over the Briarwood. Stand in one and tap `A` to attune it. Stand in any attuned ring to **travel** between rings. Some rings want an offering or a puzzle solved first.


**Burrows.** Hidden one-room caves behind something that looks like scenery. If a wall of the map looks wrong, try it.


**Tale Pages (100).** Lost pages of the Book. Some lie about, some are in chests, some are rewards. Collect all 100 for a surprise.


**Charms (60).** Small cosmetic keepsakes found in chests, burrows, quests and secrets. Wear them from the Wardrobe in the pause menu. They do not change the fight, only how you look doing it.


**Turnstones.** Collect them for a record of your progress between chapters.


**Chests** pay coins *and* something else: a jar, Gingerbombs, a full heal. Chests that look unreachable are reachable by a tool you will find later. **Come back**.


**Signs and NPCs** read differently in the two worlds. Talk to everyone twice, once in each.


> **Tip:** The side quests (orphans, brothers, the foreman, the library) pay the best. Anyone who asks for help three times is telling you something.

## 7. Shops and Seasons

*Madam Thimble sells secrets, not goods. Mostly.*

**Farthings** are your coins. Your **purse** only holds so many, so spend before it overflows ("purse full"). Upgrade it at the shop.


**Madam Thimble** sells jars (Broth heals, Moonwater refills Ink, a Wish Jar saves you once), better Quills and Books, and rumours. You can carry four jars.


**The Wheel** cycles the seasons in the Briarwood. Dormant, Spring, Summer, Autumn and later Winter each change the same room in a different way. A frozen lake you cannot cross in Spring is a road in Winter.


**Cold.** Frozen places hurt you slowly. Keep moving, stay near braziers, and find something warm.


**Deep water.** Late in the game you can swim, but you cannot breathe. Bubbles (◌) are air pockets. Stand in one to refill.


**Wardrobe** (pause menu): swap cloaks and wear your Charms.

## 8. Tips and Secrets

*Things we would tell you if we were not the Narrator.*

• Read every sign twice (Storybook and Truth). Some of them are lying and some are confessing.


• If a puzzle looks impossible, you are probably missing a tool, a season, or a world. Leave and come back; the game is built for that.


• Bombs reveal more than cracked walls. Hit suspicious dead ends.


• Hold **K** to Read when you have Blot. Fake walls and hidden things shimmer.


• You cannot lose a tool or a page. Keys are spent on locked doors, so use them with intent. Do not be afraid to experiment otherwise.


• Shielding while walking is slow but safe. Shielding against a reflected shot (Atlas or better) can **send it back**.


• Sleeping things stay asleep until you get close. Take a breath before you do.


• Broth before a boss. Moonwater before a puzzle that uses Blot. Nothing is saved for later.


• Stuck for a destination? Memory Stones warm when you touch them and tell you where to go next, and Madam Thimble sells rumours.


• The pause menu shows what you hold and your totals: Charms, Pages, Turnstones, Memories, Mirror Pieces. Use it as a checklist.

## 9. After the Tale

*The book has more than one last page.*

There are **five endings**. Some are kind. One is a joke. One is a footnote. Reaching one does not close your save.


After your first ending the **Last Page** waits in the final room. Talk to her to choose another ending, or to enter the **Boss Rush**.


**Boss Rush: the Narrator's Greatest Hits.** Every boss and mini-boss in the book, back to back, on a **copy** of your save, so nothing you do changes your real game. You heal a little between fights, dying ends the run, and your best time is kept. First clear pays farthings.


> **Tip:** The Narrator is not your enemy. He is something sadder than that, and he is very fond of you.
